about me

I’m a singer, lyricist and producer based in Germany. My journey as a vocalist started over more than a decade ago in 2013. At that time I was knee deep into anime, vocaloid and a dream: to understand what my favorite songs were about with little to no english comprehension. With that in mind, my 14 year old self dedicated her free time to writing, recording, mixing and sharing creativity with the world.

Fast forward to now, I’ve created over 200 covers in either english, german or japanese, performed live at MagFest and Connichi, collaborated with people I’ve never dared to dream of collaborating with and reached over 84 million views across a variety of streaming platforms.

That being said, none of this would've been possible without the immense support of each and everyone of you out there that listens and streams my music. You are a massive part of why I have no means of stopping and facing head on what the future's got in store for us.

I can’t wait for what's yet to come and can't thank you enough for being part of this journey.

As always, with lots of love,

Yours, jen x
