about me

I’m a singer, lyricist and producer based in Germany. My journey as a vocalist started over more than a decade ago in 2013. At that time I was knee deep into anime, vocaloid and a dream: to understand what my favorite songs were about with little to no english comprehension. With that in mind, my 14 year old self dedicated her free time to writing, recording, mixing and sharing creativity with the world.

Fast forward to now, I’ve created over 200 covers, collaborated with people I’ve never thought I’d collaborate with and reached over 84 million people with my music; and I have no means of stopping.

I can’t wait for what the futures holds and thank you immensly for being part of this journey.

As always, with lots of love,

Yours, jen x
